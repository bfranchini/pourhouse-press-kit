Read the room. Pull the taps. Survive the rush.

Pourhouse is a bartending game about keeping everything together when the rush hits. Orders stack up. Patience runs out. Somebody always wants one more.

The Tavern

It gets loud
in here.

Every patron has a patience meter, a temper, and an order. Let one run dry and they walk. Keep them happy and the tips keep coming.

How it plays

01

Beat the clock

A shift is a timer, a score, and a bar full of people who all want serving first. Miss the goal and the night is a bust. Hit it and the next one gets harder.

02

Then beat the expert

Clearing a level is the easy part. Every shift keeps a second, harder target and remembers your personal best, so there is always a reason to pour it again.

03

Learn the regulars

Some customers tip big. Some nurse one drink all night. And some, as the game puts it, just don't know when to quit.
